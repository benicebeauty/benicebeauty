The Vision & The Team

Cultivating Timeless Beauty

Our Sanctuary

Where Clinical Precision Meets Botanical Luxury

At BE NICE BEAUTY, we look at the skin as a living, breathing canvas. We believe that true dermal health cannot be achieved through aggressive, short-term interventions. Instead, we combine advanced, evidence-based medical diagnostics with deeply nurturing botanical rituals.

"We do not aim to alter your features; our mission is to cultivate your skin’s inherent, timeless vitality."

Every treatment in our Fitzrovia sanctuary is fully customized to your skin’s unique, evolving parameters, ensuring profound longevity and a naturally sophisticated glow.

The Expertise

Meet Edit Balla

Founder & Specialist Nurse Practitioner

With over a decade of clinical experience in advanced aesthetics and critical medical fields, Edit Balla founded BE NICE BEAUTY to build a bridge between rigorous scientific standards and a deeply holistic client experience.

"Skincare is a continuous dialogue between the practitioner and the skin canvas. It requires absolute clinical integrity."

As a Specialist Nurse Practitioner, Edit treats every face with artistic respect and uncompromising safety, curating bespoke journeys that empower you to look and feel completely at peace within your own skin.
