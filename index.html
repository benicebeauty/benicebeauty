BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A little clarity.
A little reassurance.

A few helpful answers to make planning your visit feel simple.

Have another question? Get in touch ↗
01

How can I book an appointment?

You can book online through Fresha, where you’ll find our treatments, prices and available appointments. If you have a question before booking, you’re welcome to contact us by phone or email.

02

Can you help me choose the right treatment?

Of course. If you’re unsure where to start, get in touch with Edit to discuss your skin concerns and what you’d like to achieve. She’ll help you explore suitable options and let you know whether a consultation is needed before booking.

03

Will my treatment always be with Edit?

Yes. Every treatment at Be Nice Beauty is carried out by Edit, so you’ll see the same dedicated practitioner each time. This continuity allows her to get to know your preferences and provide care tailored to you.

04

Do I need a consultation before my treatment?

Some treatments require a consultation before your first appointment so Edit can assess suitability and discuss what to expect. A patch test may also be needed for certain services. Please check the treatment information or contact us before booking if you’re unsure.

05

How should I prepare for my first visit?

Preparation depends on the treatment you’ve chosen. Please check any instructions provided with your booking, and contact us if anything is unclear. At your first visit, Edit will discuss your goals and any relevant health information before proceeding with your treatment.

06

How can I change or cancel my appointment?

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel your appointment. Check your Fresha booking confirmation for the cancellation terms that apply to your booking, including any notice period or fees.
