01

How does a chemical peel work?

A chemical peel uses a carefully selected solution to exfoliate the skin. The depth and intensity of this process depend on the formulation and how it is applied.

As the skin recovers, its appearance and texture may improve. The aim is to address selected concerns while respecting your skin’s suitability and recovery needs.

Peeling is not a measure of success on its own. A more intensive treatment is not automatically a better choice for your skin.

02

What may a peel help with?

Depending on the peel and your individual skin, treatment may improve the appearance of:

  • Uneven skin tone and selected pigmentation concerns.
  • Rough or uneven texture.
  • Fine lines.
  • Some acne-related concerns and superficial marks.

Results vary, and some concerns require a series of treatments or a different approach. A peel should not be expected to remove deep facial folds or significantly lift sagging skin.

During your consultation, discuss the changes you would like to see and what the proposed treatment can realistically achieve.

03

Understanding peel depths.

Superficial peels

These act mainly on the outer skin layers. They may suit selected surface concerns, but redness, sensitivity or flaking can still occur.

Medium-depth peels

These reach deeper than superficial peels and generally involve more visible peeling and a longer recovery period. They require careful assessment and follow-up care.

Deep peels

Deep peels are intensive medical procedures requiring specialist care and substantial recovery. They are different from a routine salon facial peel.

The treatment details matter

An ingredient name alone does not establish a peel’s depth. Formulation, concentration and application all influence the treatment. This overview explains the categories; it does not mean that every category is offered at Be Nice Beauty.

04

Preparation and aftercare.

Tell your practitioner about your medical history, medication, skin sensitivity and recent treatments. Ask which peel is proposed, why it suits your concerns, and what recovery and follow-up it requires.

Follow the preparation and aftercare instructions provided for your treatment. Avoid picking peeling skin, protect it from the sun, and ask when to resume active skin-care products.

Chemical peels carry risks, including irritation, changes in pigmentation, infection and scarring. Contact your practitioner promptly if you experience unexpected or worsening symptoms.

At Be Nice Beauty, we can discuss your goals and explain the available chemical peel treatment and its suitability for your skin.
