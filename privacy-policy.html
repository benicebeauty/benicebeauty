This policy covers our website, enquiries, appointments and treatment-related processing. Our services are for adults aged 18 and over.

02

Information we collect

Depending on your interaction with us and the treatment you book, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Your date of birth and gender where requested and relevant to your consultation.
  • Appointment details, treatment history, enquiries and correspondence.
  • Skin concerns, treatment goals, skincare products and relevant previous treatments.
  • Relevant health information, including answers about medical conditions, medication, allergies, pregnancy, breastfeeding and treatment contraindications.
  • Patch test information, results, treatment notes and consent records.
  • Agreed treatment photographs and marketing preferences.
  • Booking, payment status and transaction information available through Fresha.

Answers to health questions may be health information even when the answer is simply yes or no.

Our hosting provider may also process technical information, such as IP addresses and server request details, to operate and protect the website.

We may display selected public client reviews with the reviewer's published name and source attribution.

03

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange appointments.
  • Assess treatment suitability, identify precautions and provide personalised treatments.
  • Maintain consultation, patch test, consent and treatment records.
  • Provide booking confirmations, preparation instructions and aftercare.
  • Document treatment progress.
  • Manage transactions, cancellations, complaints and potential insurance or legal claims.
  • Send promotional emails where you have opted in.
  • Share selected public reviews to help prospective clients understand our services.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.

Please avoid sending detailed medical information through the website contact form. Contact us so we can guide you through the appropriate consultation process.

04

Our legal grounds

Contract: to arrange and provide services, including steps you ask us to take before booking.

Legitimate interests: to respond to general enquiries, manage our business, protect the website, maintain appropriate records, display relevant public reviews and handle complaints or claims. We consider your rights and interests when relying on this ground.

Legal obligations: to retain or disclose information where required by applicable law.

Consent: for optional promotional emails and other uses where consent is required.

Health information receives additional protection. We obtain your explicit consent to process relevant health information for treatment suitability assessments and related treatment documentation, including documentary photographs where they reveal health information. This is separate from your consent to receive treatment.

Where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, we may process relevant health information under the legal claims condition.

You can withdraw consent by contacting us. This does not affect previous lawful processing. If information necessary to assess treatment safety cannot be processed, we may be unable to provide the treatment. Some records may still need to be retained on another applicable legal ground.

Treatment photographs

Where agreed, photographs taken before, during or after treatment are used solely to document treatment and progress. They are stored in Fresha alongside treatment records.

We do not use these photographs on our website, social media or in advertising. Any proposed future promotional use would require a separate, optional agreement.

07

Marketing messages

We send promotional emails about special offers, treatments and salon news through Fresha where you have opted in. Marketing consent is optional and separate from treatment and health information consent.

You can unsubscribe using the link in each promotional email or by contacting us. We remove those who unsubscribe from the marketing sending list.

08

Sharing and security

Information may be processed by Fresha and its service providers, and by Hostinger for website hosting and email services. Where necessary, relevant information may also be shared with our insurer, legal or professional advisers, or authorities where disclosure is legally required.

We limit disclosure to what is needed for the relevant purpose. We do not sell personal information.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where required, transfers must be protected by an appropriate mechanism, such as a UK adequacy decision or recognised contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your data.

09

How long we keep information

Treatment documentation
Consultation forms, relevant health information, consent records, patch test results, treatment notes and documentary photographs are normally retained for five years after your last treatment.
Routine enquiries
Simple enquiries that do not become part of a treatment record are normally deleted 12 months after the last substantive communication.
Payments and accounting
Relevant transaction records are retained for the period needed to meet applicable accounting, tax and legal requirements. Fresha applies its own retention arrangements to data it holds for its independent purposes.
Marketing preferences
Marketing details are retained while needed for permitted marketing. After an opt-out, a minimal suppression record may be retained to prevent further marketing.
Technical information and public reviews
Technical information is retained while necessary for website operation and security. Selected review content is kept while relevant to our website and reviewed when updated or when a concern is raised.

Relevant records may be kept longer where necessary for a complaint, insurance matter, legal claim or legal obligation. At the end of the applicable period, we review records and securely delete or anonymise information no longer needed.

10

Cookies and website services

We do not use Google Analytics on this website. Our website's JavaScript does not use local storage, session storage or set its own cookies.

Enquiry form security

When our enquiry form is enabled, loading the form sets a necessary first-party session cookie called bn_contact_session. It helps protect the form against unauthorised submissions.

The cookie contains a randomly generated session identifier, not your name, email address or message. It is used for form security, not advertising or visitor analytics. Blocking this cookie prevents the enquiry form from working; you can contact us by email or telephone instead.

This is a session cookie with no fixed expiry date. It normally lasts for your browser session, although browser session-restoration settings may preserve it. The form's submission security token expires after one hour and is invalidated after a successful submission. This token expiry does not itself delete the cookie.

Protection against spam

To limit repeated submissions, our server keeps attempt counters and timestamps associated with a pseudonymous identifier derived from your IP address. The counter file does not contain your name, email address, message or the original IP address.

Counter entries expire one hour after their counting period begins. Expired entries are removed when the counter file is next updated during a permitted submission attempt; they are not automatically deleted at the exact moment the hour ends.

Our hosting and email providers also process technical information needed to deliver messages, operate the website and protect their services, such as connection information and server logs.

Fonts and icons

Google Fonts and an icon stylesheet from Cloudflare's cdnjs service load when you visit the website. These requests disclose technical information, including your IP address and browser request details, to the respective provider.

Google Maps

The embedded map loads only after you choose to load it. Doing so connects your browser to Google, which may process device information and use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. You can leave the map unloaded and use the displayed address instead.

Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab)

External links

Following links to Fresha, Google, Instagram or Facebook takes you to external services governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect services you choose to use.
