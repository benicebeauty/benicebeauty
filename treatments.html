Your facial questions.

Which facial is right for my skin?

The right facial depends on your skin’s needs and what you’d like to achieve, whether that’s hydration, a brighter complexion or help with congestion. How should I prepare for my facial?

Preparation can vary depending on your chosen facial. Before your appointment, let Edit know about any recent skin treatments, irritation or products you’re using that may affect your skin. If you’re unsure whether to adjust your skincare routine, please contact us for treatment-specific advice.

Aftercare depends on the facial you’ve received and how your skin responds. Edit will explain how to care for your skin, including any products or activities to avoid and when to resume your usual routine. Before your treatment.

01

Do I need a consultation first?

A consultation is required at least 48 hours before your first microneedling, RF microneedling or facial mesotherapy treatment. This gives Edit time to discuss your goals, review relevant health information and assess whether the treatment is suitable for you. If you’re considering another aesthetic treatment and are unsure about the requirements, please contact us before booking.

02

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Preparation depends on your chosen treatment. Please tell Edit about any medications, skincare products, recent treatments or changes to your health that may be relevant. She’ll explain any preparation needed before your appointment. Do not stop prescribed medication without advice from your prescribing clinician.

03

What should I expect after my treatment?

Recovery varies depending on the treatment and your individual skin response. Edit will explain any expected temporary effects, whether you should allow time for recovery and how to care for the treated area. You’ll also receive guidance on when to resume your usual skincare routine and when to seek further advice if you have concerns.

A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Before your wax.

01

How long should my hair be for waxing?

As a general guide, allow your hair to grow to around 6 mm — approximately a quarter of an inch — so the wax can grip it effectively. If you’ve recently shaved or are unsure whether your hair is long enough, contact us before your appointment for advice.

02

How should I prepare for my wax?

Arrive with the treatment area clean and avoid applying heavy creams or oils immediately beforehand. Let Edit know about any skin irritation, recent treatments or medication that may affect your skin. If your skin is sore, sunburnt or damaged, please contact us before attending.

03

What should I avoid after waxing?

Freshly waxed skin can be temporarily sensitive. Avoid hot baths and showers, saunas, vigorous exercise and friction from tight clothing while the area settles. Keep your skincare gentle and avoid exfoliating irritated skin. Edit will explain how long to follow these precautions and when to resume your usual routine.
