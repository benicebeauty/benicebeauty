RF Microneedling
Treatment.

Understanding the treatment, its potential benefits, and what to expect as your skin recovers.

From Be Nice Beauty · Fitzrovia, London

Published 21 February 2024 · Updated 7 October 2026

In this article

How it works Potential benefits Recovery & results Your consultation

01

How does RF microneedling work?

Radiofrequency microneedling combines fine needles with radiofrequency energy. The needles create controlled punctures in the skin, while RF energy delivers heat beneath the surface.

The treatment aims to encourage the skin’s repair response and collagen remodelling. The device, treatment settings and suitability assessment all influence the outcome.

02

What may the treatment help with?

Depending on your skin and the treatment plan, RF microneedling may help improve the appearance of:

  • Fine lines and wrinkles.
  • Acne scarring and uneven skin texture.
  • Mild skin laxity.
  • Visible pores.

Results vary between individuals. A consultation helps establish realistic expectations and whether this approach is appropriate for your concerns.

03

Recovery & results.

Immediately after treatment

Redness, swelling, tenderness or a feeling of tightness may occur. Their intensity and duration depend on your skin and the treatment settings.

During recovery

Follow the aftercare instructions provided for your treatment, including guidance on skin care, sun protection and returning to your usual activities. Recovery should be assessed individually rather than assumed to follow a fixed timetable.

Over the following weeks and months

Changes may develop gradually as the skin remodels. Further sessions may be discussed after reviewing your response; neither the number of sessions nor the results are the same for everyone.

Understanding the risks

RF microneedling carries risks. Serious complications reported with certain uses include burns, scarring, fat loss and nerve damage. Discuss the benefits, risks and alternatives with your practitioner before deciding on treatment.

If you experience unexpected or worsening symptoms, seek advice from your practitioner or an appropriate medical professional.

04

Start with a conversation.

At Be Nice Beauty, your consultation is an opportunity to discuss your concerns, medical history and skin goals, and to explore a suitable treatment plan.

A consultation is required at least 48 hours before your first RF microneedling treatment.
