The Beauty Journal · Skin care

The Benefits of
Facial Treatments.

Considered care for your skin, and a little time to slow down.

From Be Nice Beauty · Fitzrovia, London

Published 11 January 2024 · Updated 7 October 2026

In this article

Personalised care Cleansing & hydration Time for yourself Beyond your appointment

01

A facial shaped around your skin.

A professional facial can combine targeted skin care with an opportunity to relax. Its value comes from choosing suitable products and techniques for your individual needs.

Rather than following the same routine for every client, a personalised treatment considers how your skin feels, your concerns and what you would like to achieve.

At Be Nice Beauty, Edith takes time to understand your skin and explain the proposed treatment. This conversation helps establish realistic expectations before your facial begins.

02

Cleansing, comfort and hydration.

Thoughtful cleansing

Cleansing removes surface impurities and prepares the skin for the next treatment steps. The approach should suit your skin rather than leave it feeling stripped or uncomfortable.

Exfoliation where appropriate

Some facials include exfoliation to improve surface smoothness and the appearance of dullness. It is not necessary in every treatment: sensitive or irritated skin may need a gentler approach.

Hydration and moisture

Suitable masks, serums and moisturisers can help the skin feel softer and more comfortable. Product selection matters more than the number of steps or the strength of the ingredients.

The effects depend on the facial you choose and your skin’s response. A refreshed appearance after treatment does not mean that every concern has been permanently resolved.

03

Time to pause.

For many clients, the experience matters as much as the skin-care steps. A quiet setting, attentive care and unhurried treatment can make an appointment feel like time set aside entirely for yourself.

Where appropriate, facial massage adds a comforting, hands-on element to the session. Your preferences and comfort guide the experience.

Whether you enjoy an occasional facial or choose ongoing appointments, the rhythm should suit your needs and circumstances.

04

Care beyond your appointment.

A facial is also an opportunity to ask questions about your routine. Discuss the products you use, any sensitivity you notice and changes in your skin.

Practical advice can help you make considered choices between appointments, including suitable cleansing, moisturising and sun protection. More treatments or more products are not automatically better.

When your skin needs medical advice

Persistent acne, rosacea or other diagnosed skin conditions may require care from a dermatologist. A cosmetic facial should not replace prescribed treatment.

Tell your practitioner about medication, recent procedures and any irritation before your appointment.

At Be Nice Beauty, you can explore facial treatments with one dedicated practitioner, with care adapted to your skin and your goals.
