02

Bookings & payments

Online bookings are made through Fresha. Please check your selected treatment, appointment time, price and booking conditions before confirming.

We do not require an advance deposit. Fresha requires a payment card to secure your booking. This card guarantee is separate from an advance payment for your treatment.

Payment for your treatment is due at the end of your appointment and is made by card. Prices are shown in pounds sterling.

Any applicable cancellation, missed-appointment or late-arrival charge will be handled using the payment arrangements agreed during booking, subject to section 6.

Promotional prices apply only where the offer is valid for your booking and its conditions are met. Any agreed change to your treatment or its price will be explained before the treatment proceeds.

03

Changing your appointment

You may reschedule your appointment without a rescheduling charge by giving at least 24 hours’ notice before your appointment starts.

Changes are subject to appointment availability. Please use the options provided in your Fresha booking confirmation or contact us before the 24-hour deadline.

Guest rescheduling is not available less than 24 hours before the appointment. If you can no longer attend, please contact us promptly. The cancellation or missed-appointment conditions below may apply.

04

Cancellations & missed appointments

Your appointment reserves dedicated treatment time with Edit. Please give as much notice as possible if you cannot attend.

  • At least 24 hours’ notice: cancellation is free of charge.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: a cancellation charge of up to 50% of the booked treatment price applies.
  • Missed appointment without cancellation: a charge of up to 100% of the booked treatment price applies.

These percentages are maximum charges. The amount payable is subject to the loss assessment explained in section 6 and any applicable statutory cancellation rights in section 10.

To cancel, use the cancellation option in your Fresha booking confirmation where available, or contact us by telephone or email. We use the time your cancellation reaches Fresha or us to calculate the notice given.

05

Late arrivals

Please arrive on time so that you can enjoy your full appointment. If you expect to be late, contact us as soon as possible.

If you arrive up to 15 minutes late, your treatment time will be shortened to keep subsequent appointments on schedule. The full booked treatment price remains payable, subject to section 6 in respect of any treatment time not provided.

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we will not carry out the treatment. This is treated as a missed appointment, with a charge of up to 100% of the booked treatment price, subject to section 6.

We will not rush a treatment or omit essential safety steps to accommodate a late arrival.

06

How appointment charges apply

Charges for cancellations, missed appointments and treatment time lost through late arrival are intended to cover our reasonable direct loss from the reserved appointment.

We take account of costs saved and any replacement booking for the same appointment time, and take reasonable steps to reduce our loss.

Where our loss is lower than the stated maximum charge, we reduce the charge accordingly. We do not recover the same loss twice. If there is no remaining loss, no charge for the unused appointment time is payable.

For a shortened treatment, the total of the treatment charge and any charge for unused appointment time will not exceed the original booked treatment price.

You may contact us for an explanation of a charge. These terms do not override your statutory rights.

07

Treatment suitability

Some treatments require a consultation, a patch test or both before the treatment appointment. We will explain any requirements that apply to your chosen treatment.

Please provide accurate, relevant information about allergies, medication, pregnancy, skin conditions, recent treatments and any other circumstances that may affect treatment suitability.

Please also tell Edit about changes since your previous consultation. This helps us assess suitability and plan your care.

We may recommend a different treatment or decline to proceed where the booked treatment is unsuitable. Any alternative and its price will be discussed with you before you decide.

A consultation or patch test does not guarantee that a reaction will not occur.

If a treatment cannot proceed for safety reasons, we will explain the next steps. Any proposed charge must be justified by the circumstances and comply with section 6 and your statutory rights.

Individual responses and results vary. Descriptions, photographs and guest reviews illustrate possible experiences and do not guarantee an identical outcome.

Please follow the preparation and aftercare instructions provided for your treatment. If anything is unclear, ask Edit before proceeding.

If you have concerns about your skin or your response after a treatment, contact us promptly. Seek medical attention where needed, particularly for a severe or worsening reaction.

Nothing in these terms requires you to waive your rights or releases us from responsibility where the law does not permit this.

This does not limit any other rights you may have under consumer law.

Statutory cancellation rights

Where statutory distance-contract cancellation rights apply to an online or telephone service booking, you may cancel without giving a reason within 14 days, beginning the day after the contract is made.

Tell us by email, telephone or post using a clear statement. You may use the optional form below. Send your notice before the cancellation period ends.
