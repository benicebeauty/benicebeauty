What is a
Microcurrent Facial?

A closer look at microcurrent facial care, hands-on treatment and realistic expectations.

From Be Nice Beauty · Fitzrovia, London

Published 11 February 2024 · Updated 7 October 2026

In this article

How it works Microcurrent gloves What to expect Personalised care

01

What is a microcurrent facial?

A microcurrent facial uses low-level electrical currents applied through a device in contact with the skin. It is a non-invasive approach often offered as part of facial toning treatments.

You may have heard it described as a “workout for your face”. This is a useful analogy, but it should not be understood as a promise to restore facial muscles to their original position or to produce the results of a surgical lift.

The treatment experience and visible results vary according to your skin, the device used and the approach taken by your practitioner.

02

A hands-on approach.

At Be Nice Beauty, our Microcurrent Face Contour Massage combines manual facial massage with Zemits conductive microcurrent gloves.

Instead of working solely with handheld probes, the practitioner uses conductive gloves to deliver low-level electrical stimulation while carrying out controlled massage movements.

This allows the session to combine the experience of facial massage with microcurrent technology. The treatment is adapted to your needs and comfort following a suitability assessment.

03

What can you expect?

During your appointment

Your practitioner will explain the treatment and check your comfort throughout. Sensations can vary; let your practitioner know immediately if anything feels uncomfortable.

Results and expectations

Microcurrent treatments are commonly offered with the aim of improving the appearance of facial tone and definition. Visible changes are individual and should not be considered guaranteed or permanent.

We will discuss your goals and review your response before recommending further sessions. There is no single treatment schedule that is right for everyone.

After your treatment

The treatment does not involve needles or surgical incisions. However, “non-invasive” does not mean that side effects are impossible. Follow the advice provided for your skin and contact your practitioner if you experience unexpected symptoms.

Is microcurrent suitable for everyone?

Suitability must be assessed before treatment. Tell your practitioner about your medical history, pregnancy, implanted electronic devices such as a pacemaker, and any recent facial procedures.

These details help your practitioner check the device instructions and determine whether treatment is appropriate or whether medical advice is needed first.

04

Care shaped around you.

Choosing a facial starts with understanding what you would like to achieve and what is suitable for your skin.

At Be Nice Beauty, Edith takes the time to discuss your concerns and explain the available options. If you are interested in microcurrent facial care, contact us to discuss the treatment and your individual needs.
